# 4. Interview Guide

| | |
|---|---|
| **YouTrack task** | [DOA-4](https://yeralytemir.youtrack.cloud/issue/DOA-4) |
| **Format** | Semi-structured interview, 30–40 minutes, online or in person |
| **Roles** | Interviewer (Ansar); note-taker if available (another team member) |

## 4.1 Rules for the interviewer

1. Ask about **past behaviour**, not hypothetical future use: "Tell me about the last time you..." rather than "Would you use...?".
2. Do not describe the proposed solution before Section E. Earlier sections must show the problem in the participant's own words.
3. Do not lead: avoid "Don't you think that...?" and avoid agreeing or disagreeing with answers.
4. Follow up with "Why?", "Can you give an example?", "What did you do then?".
5. Write down the participant's wording for important statements. Mark your own interpretations with **[I]** so that facts and interpretations stay separate.
6. If the participant starts describing a medical condition or asks for medical advice, say that the interview is not a consultation and recommend a doctor.
7. Fill in the record template ([05-results-template.md](05-results-template.md)) within 24 hours.

## 4.2 User interview

The ID in brackets shows which assumption from [02-assumptions-register.md](02-assumptions-register.md) each question tests.

### A. Introduction and consent (3 min)

- Introduce yourself and the project in one sentence without describing the solution.
- Read the consent text (Section 3.4) and record the answers.

### E. Reaction to the concept (8 min)

Now describe the concept in neutral terms: *"We are designing an app that looks at what you eat, how much you move and how your weight changes, shows which of these factors matter most for you, and gives weekly nutrition and activity recommendations. Each recommendation explains the rule it is based on."*

| ID | Question (EN) | Вопрос (RU) | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q13 | Which part of this, if any, would be useful for you? Which would not? Why? | Какая часть этого, если такая есть, была бы вам полезна? Какая нет? Почему? | A6 |
| Q14 | Would you prefer food and activity advice in one app or in separate apps? Why? | Вы бы предпочли рекомендации по питанию и активности в одном приложении или в разных? Почему? | A3 |
| Q15 | The app explains why it gives each recommendation. Does that matter to you? When have you trusted or not trusted advice from an app or a person? | Приложение объясняет, почему даёт каждую рекомендацию. Важно ли это для вас? Когда вы доверяли или не доверяли советам приложения или человека? | A4 |
| Q16 | Imagine the app suggests "walk 500 more steps than last week" instead of "walk 10,000 steps". How would you react? | Представьте, что приложение предлагает «пройти на 500 шагов больше, чем на прошлой неделе» вместо «пройти 10 000 шагов». Как бы вы к этому отнеслись? | A7 |
| Q17 | How do you feel about reminders from apps? Which reminders do you keep and which do you switch off? | Как вы относитесь к напоминаниям от приложений? Какие оставляете, а какие отключаете? | A8 |

### F. Data, privacy and safety (5 min)

| ID | Question (EN) | Вопрос (RU) | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q18 | Which data would you be willing to enter or share: food, weight once a week, waist size, steps, heart rate from a watch? Which would you not? | Какие данные вы были бы готовы вводить или передавать: питание, вес раз в неделю, обхват талии, шаги, пульс с часов? Какие нет? | A9, A11 |
| Q19 | Does it worry you where data about your weight and health are stored? Has that ever stopped you from using an app? | Беспокоит ли вас, где хранятся данные о вашем весе и здоровье? Останавливало ли это вас когда-нибудь от использования приложения? | A10 |
| Q20 | Before giving advice, the app asks a few health-safety questions and, in some cases, recommends seeing a doctor. How would you feel about that? | Перед тем как давать рекомендации, приложение задаёт несколько вопросов о здоровье и в некоторых случаях советует обратиться к врачу. Как вы к этому отнесётесь? | A12 |

### G. Closing (2 min)

- "Is there anything important about managing weight that I did not ask about?" / «Есть ли что-то важное про управление весом, о чём я не спросил?»
- "Would you agree to test a prototype later?" / «Согласились бы вы позже протестировать прототип?» (record yes/no only; contact details stay outside the repository).
- Thank the participant.

## 4.3 Expert interview (dietitian, doctor or fitness trainer)

| ID | Question (EN) | Вопрос (RU) | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|

| X3 | Which recommendations can safely be given to adults with BMI 25–34.9 without individual supervision? Which cannot? | Какие рекомендации можно безопасно давать взрослым с ИМТ 25–34,9 без индивидуального наблюдения? Какие нельзя? | E1 |
| X4 | What weekly rate of weight loss and what activity progression would you consider safe for this group? | X5 | Our app excludes people with diabetes, cardiovascular disease, pregnancy, weight-loss medication or PAR-Q+ contraindications. Чего-то не хватает? | E2 |
| X6 | Which local foods or dishes must a food database include to be realistic for Kazakhstan? | Какие местные продукты и блюда обязательно должны быть в базе продуктов, чтобы она была реалистичной для Казахстана? | E3 |
| X7 | Why do your clients usually stop following recommendations? What helps them continue? | Почему ваши клиенты обычно перестают следовать рекомендациям? Что помогает им продолжать? | E4 |
| X8 | Would you be willing to review our rule base later (for example, by e-mail)? | Были бы вы готовы позже посмотреть нашу базу правил (например, по почте)? | Follow-up |

A "yes" to X8 is a strong form of evidence for the committee (a substantive agreement with an external expert). Record it in writing (e-mail or message) and keep a screenshot outside the repository; note the date in the interview record.
