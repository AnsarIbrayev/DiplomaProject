## 5.1 Data-protection rules for records

- Use participant codes only: **P1–P5** for users, **X1** for the expert.
- Do **not** write names, phone numbers, usernames, exact weight, exact age, employer or diagnoses.
- Keep consent confirmations, contact details and audio recordings **outside the repository** (for example, on the interviewer's university drive). Delete audio after the notes are checked.
- Paraphrase rather than quote if a quotation could identify the participant.
- If the participant did not consent to quotations (consent question 3), do not use quotation marks for their words.

## 5.3 Expert record

Use the same metadata table, replacing the participant fields with: **profession**, **years of experience**, **workplace type** (clinic, private practice, gym) and **consent to be named in the thesis** (yes / no). Replace the assumption check with E1–E4 and add the answer to X8 (willing to review the rule base).
